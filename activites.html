Hiking & Nature

Lovćen National Park

Scenic mountain trails offer a memorable experience of nature with panoramic view on Kotor Bay. Also, you have a chance and a visit to the Mausoleum of Petar Petrović Njegoš II on Mount Lovćen, with breathtaking views stretching for miles.

Durmitor Adventure

Durmitor is well known for hiking among mountain peaks, interesting biodiversity and glacial lakes such as Black Lake. Also, part of this National Park is Tara River Canyon, one of the deepest canyons in Europe and a UNESCO-protected wonder.

Skadarsko Lake

Skadarsko Lake is known for stunning natural beauty, breathtaking surroundings and rich birdlife. You can take a boat trips where you can see interesting ecosystem of this Lake which is part of National Park.

Prokletije Mountains

Prokletije are mountains known for their dramatic peaks and untouched wilderness that make them some of the most challenging hiking routes in the region. If you are experienced hiker, You have a chance to enjoy breathtaking landscapes.

Water Activities

Kayaking on Skadar Lake

This tour is escape into peaceful area with beautiful nature and diverse birdlife. You have a chance to see small fishing villages, floating water lilies and views of surrounding mountains.

Rafting on Tara River

The Tara River, often called the “Tear of Europe,” is famous for its crystal-clear waters and breathtaking scenery. If you’re looking for an adrenaline boost, you should definitely try a rafting tour through this stunning, untouched natural environment.

Nevidio Canyoning

Known as one of the last discovered canyons in Europe, Nevidio offers a mix of narrow passages, waterfalls, and natural pools hidden deep within untouched nature. As you move through it—you’ll experience an unforgettable raw beauty.

Blue Cave

Famous for its glowing turquoise-blue water, Blue Cave creates a magical atmosphere when sunlight reflects through the sea entrance. Visitors can enter the cave by boat and swim in its crystal-clear waters, making it a truly unforgettable experience.

History & Culture

Kotor Old Town Walking Tour

Explore medieval streets, hidden squares and city walls while learning about UNESCO-protected history and Venetian heritage.

Ostrog Monastery Visit

A cultural and spiritual experience visiting one of the most important monasteries in the Balkans, built into a dramatic cliff.

Perast Boat Tour

Take a scenic boat ride to the island church of Our Lady of the Rocks and discover baroque coastal architecture.

Old Bar Heritage Walk

Walk through ancient ruins of Stari Bar and explore layers of Ottoman, Venetian, and medieval history with a guide.
