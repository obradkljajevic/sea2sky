Hiking & Nature

Lovćen National Park

Scenic mountain trails offer a memorable experience of nature with panoramic view on Kotor Bay. Also, you have a chance and a visit to the Mausoleum of Petar Petrović Njegoš II on Mount Lovćen, with breathtaking views stretching for miles.

Durmitor Adventure

Durmitor is well known for hiking among mountain peaks, interesting biodiversity and glacial lakes such as Black Lake. Also, part of this National Park is Tara River Canyon, one of the deepest canyons in Europe and a UNESCO-protected wonder.

Skadarsko Lake

Skadarsko Lake is known for stunning natural beauty, breathtaking surroundings and rich birdlife. You can take a boat trips where you can see interesting ecosystem of this Lake which is part of National Park.

Prokletije Mountains

Prokletije are mountains known for their dramatic peaks and untouched wilderness that make them some of the most challenging hiking routes in the region. If you are experienced hiker, You have a chance to enjoy breathtaking landscapes.
